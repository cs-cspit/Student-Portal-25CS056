Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about using the CHARUSAT CSPIT StudentHub portal, student accounts, and viewing your timetable.

1. Student Portal Accounts

How do I create or access my student portal account?

New students can fill out the Registration Form with their details. Returning students can sign in using their Student ID on the Login Page.

What should I do if I forget my account credentials?

Contact the IT support desk through the Contact Form or visit the CSE Department Academic Cell in person with your student ID card.

2. Academic Timetable & Labs

Where do B.Tech CSE Semester 3 lectures and lab sessions take place?

Theory lectures take place in Room 506. Laboratory sessions (FDSA, WDF, FCN, OOP) are conducted in computer labs 631, 632, 633, 634, and 638 as per batch allocations (A1, B1, C1).

Who are the Timetable Coordinators and Head of Department?

The HOD is Dr. Amit R Thakkar. The Timetable Coordinators are Prof. Hemang A Thakar and Prof. Avani V Khokhariya. Full faculty details are available on the About & Faculty page.
